# Where to Get Your Ice Skates Sharpened in Nashville

Start with a provider that confirms it handles your blade type. A hockey sharpening counter and a figure-skate specialist may offer different services, and a rink being open does not mean a sharpener is available. Before travelling, confirm the technician, price and collection arrangements directly.

This guide separates a current published service from an option that still needs confirmation. It also explains what to ask if you skate at [Centennial Sportsplex](/rink/centennial-sportsplex-nashville-tn), the [Ford Ice Centers](/city/tn/nashville), or in [Franklin and Williamson County](/blog/ice-skating-franklin-tn).

## A published Nashville sharpening option

Rainbo Sports lists a Nashville location and publishes competitive, recreational, coaches' and hockey sharpening services. Its [repairs and maintenance page](https://www.shoprainbo.com/repairs-maintenance/) asks customers to call the local store for prices. The [Nashville location listing](https://www.shoprainbo.com/locations/) gives the public number **615-864-7651**. These pages were checked October 16, 2026.

Ask which technician handles your blades, whether an appointment is needed and when the skates will be ready. The published service list does not establish today's staffing or guarantee a same-day handoff. If you also need mounting, fitting or repair work, describe that when you call rather than booking a sharpening and assuming the other work is included.

## Ford Ice Center pro shops: confirm the location and service

For [Bellevue](/rink/ford-ice-center-bellevue-nashville-tn) or [Antioch](/rink/ford-ice-center-antioch-antioch-tn), start with the [official Ford Ice Center site](https://www.nhl.com/predators/fordicecenter/) and contact the location. Ask whether sharpening is available for your specific figure or hockey blades, who will do it and how collection works. This review did not confirm a current location-specific sharpening price or turnaround, so do not plan around a walk-in or while-you-skate service without asking.

Public skating, academy practice ice and a pro-shop service are separate arrangements. Booking ice does not book blade work.

## Questions to ask before handing over your skates

- Do you regularly sharpen this type of figure or hockey blade?
- Can you preserve the current setup, or do you need to inspect the blade first?
- What will this work cost, including any repair or mounting work?
- Do you need an appointment, and who will do the sharpening?
- When can I collect the skates, and will that leave time to check them before my next session?

Bring any details of a previous sharpening that worked for you. If you are unsure what to request, ask the technician and your coach rather than choosing a specification from a general guide. For the basic vocabulary, see our [skate sharpening guide](/services/skate-sharpening).

### Where can I get skates sharpened in Nashville?

Rainbo publishes Nashville sharpening services and a local contact number on its official pages. Confirm the service for your blades before travelling. Ask Ford Ice locations directly about their current services; Gary Force availability remains unconfirmed in this review.

### Can I get sharpening done during a public session?

Ask the provider. An ice session reservation does not guarantee a technician, appointment or same-day collection.
